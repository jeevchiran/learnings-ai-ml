## Borrowing a pretrained classifier's opinion

<PredictReveal

Both major metrics reuse a network trained for something else entirely — an Inception classifier trained on ImageNet — and read signal off its predictions rather than off anything internal to the GAN. That reuse is what makes them applicable to any generator, regardless of architecture or training objective.

## Two metrics that disagree on purpose

<PredictReveal
  prompt="Inception Score rewards two things at once: each image getting a confident, low-entropy class prediction (quality), and the set of predicted classes across many images being diverse (coverage). FID instead compares the full distribution of Inception features for real vs. generated images. Which metric is more likely to miss a generator that memorized a handful of sharp, high-quality modes?"
  options={["FID, because it doesn't look at individual images at all", "Inception Score, because its diversity term is comparatively weak next to its per-image confidence term"]}
  correct={1}
>

<ISFIDWidget />

**Inception Score** combines per-image confidence with cross-image class diversity into a single number, but a handful of very sharp, confidently-classified modes can still produce a respectable score even with real diversity missing. **FID** instead fits a Gaussian to real-image Inception features and to generated-image Inception features, and reports the distance between the two Gaussians (the Fréchet distance) — directly comparing whole distributions rather than scoring images one at a time. Missing modes show up as real feature statistics the generated distribution simply does not reproduce, which FID is built to catch.

<FactList accent="#0369a1" items={[

---

<ConceptBox title="TL;DR">
- With no tractable likelihood, GAN quality is measured by running generated images through a separate pretrained (ImageNet) classifier and reading signal off its predictions.
- **Inception Score** (higher is better) combines per-image confidence with cross-image class diversity, but can understate mode collapse.
- **FID** (lower is better) compares the full distribution of real vs. generated Inception features directly, which makes it more sensitive to missing modes than IS.
</ConceptBox>
